Our Story

A community stage, open once again.

This historic opera house has hosted many wonderful performances over the years, and today its doors are open once again.

  • Getting kids up, out and on stage, building talent and confidence.
  • Bringing family, friends and neighbors together, just as the hall was built to do.
  • A cultural landmark on Main Street, lovingly restored.
