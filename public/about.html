About Us

Our opera house

A historic hall on Main Street, bringing live performances back to Kooskia.

History

Open once again

Built in 1920, this historic opera house has featured many amazing plays, and it's open once again!

The building was restored in 1985 and remains a cultural landmark in the city.

Our Mission

Bringing the community together

Our mission is to get kids up and out and doing things, getting a little culture in the arts, and working with the community to provide something that will allow those in the area to develop their talents and skills.

We want the venue to be used the way it was originally intended: to bring family, friends and the community together.
