# Box Office Guide

Go to **yourwebsite/admin** (there's also a "Staff sign-in" link at the bottom of
every page) and enter the password.

## Add a new show

1. Click **+ Add a New Show**.
2. Fill in the **show name**. A short tagline, description, and poster are optional
   but make the page look great. Any phone photo works.
3. Set the **Premium** and **Regular** prices, and how many of each seat to sell.
   Together they can't be more than 140.
4. Under **Dates & times**, enter the first performance's date and start time.
   Click **+ Add another showtime** for each additional performance. It copies the
   times from the row above and moves to the next day, so just adjust it.
5. Click **Save show**. It's on the website immediately.

## Change a show

Click **Edit show** on the dashboard, change anything, and click **Save show**.

- **Change a date or time:** just edit it. People who already bought tickets keep them.
  Let them know about the change.
- **Stop online sales for one performance** (for example, to sell only by phone):
  set *Online sales* to **Paused**.
- **Cancel a performance:** set it to **Cancelled**. It shows as cancelled on the website.
- **Remove a performance:** click the ✕. This only works if no tickets were sold for it.
- **Hide the whole show:** uncheck **Show on website**.

## See who's coming (guest list)

On the dashboard, click **Guest list** next to any showtime. You'll see every order
with names, seats, and order numbers.

- **Print guest list** gives you a paper list for the door.
- **Download spreadsheet** opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- At the door, tick the **In** box as each party arrives.
- **Search** by name, email, phone, or order number.

## Tickets sold at the door or by phone

On the guest list, open **Record a box-office sale or free ticket**, enter the name
and number of seats, and click **Add to guest list**. Those seats come off the website
right away, so you never oversell.

## Refunds and cancellations

On the guest list, click **Cancel** next to the order. This frees the seats so they
can be sold again. **It does not refund the card.** To refund money, open the
payment in your **Square Dashboard** (Transactions) and click **Issue refund**.

## Where's the money?

Online ticket payments go straight to your Square account, the same as any other
Square sale. Each one shows the order number (like `KOH-7F3K9Q2M`) and the buyer's name.
